I find that there are a huge number of opportunities available in Melbourne for Computer Science, Software Engineering and Web Dev type roles around, but I needed a place to record all this.

This page serves as a list of some of the most relevant and exciting developments to someone around undergraduate level with a background in CS.

Curated and updated by some of the most informed students in my networks, and constantly updated.

## Consulting

-   [DXC](http://www.dxc.technology/analytics) - DXC has a Melbourne office as well as a number abroad. It's a huge new organisation built ontop of HP
-   [PwC](https://www.pwc.com.au/consulting/data-and-analytics/meet-our-specialists.html) - I was presently suprised by the Data Science specific skills in this organisation after attending a series of talks where some of their consultants presented on new and developing tools, methods and tech in the space
-   [Servian](https://www.google.com/url?sa=t&source=web&rct=j&url=https://www.servian.com/&ved=2ahUKEwjUiY2bsJLeAhXuYt8KHUmBBpoQFjAAegQIAhAB&usg=AOvVaw1dfKydpDb0bm4boySV4Tgk) - SQL skills are key here. Talked at recent big meet with enthusiasm
-   [EY](https://www.ey.com/au/en/careers/ey-dna-careers) - One of the Big 4 has a play in the Data Space - expect a mix of traditional consulting and some software development on the implementation side
-   [Silverpond](https://silverpond.com.au/) - Melbourne based AI. They do Machine learning really well
-   [Mindfields](https://www.mindfieldsglobal.com/) - vendor-agnostic Artificial Intelligence and Robotic Process Automation services firm providing clients with Research, Training and Workshops, Consultation and Implementation.
-   [InGenious.ai](https://ingenious.ai/) - Executive staff in are active in hosting meetups in Melbourne
-   [Eliiza](https://eliiza.com.au/)

## Software Development

-   [Move37.ai](http://www.move37.ai/) - Melbourne-based startup that uses machine learning, data mining and natural language processing to augment conceptual creativity and content development (taken from website lol).
-   [Daisee](https://www.daisee.com/)
-   [QC-Technology](http://www.qc-technologies.com/)
-   [Oberix Group](http://www.oberix.com/) - IoT type stuff, more towards the smart home type applications in some of their projects
-   [Compass Education](http://compass.education/) - Supporting educational instutions. They have a really top culture around self improvement and encourage new ideas to thrive. It's been said that departments are created from pitching ideas that will support the business

## Fintech and Analytics

-   [NAB](https://www.nab.com.au/about-us/careers/graduate-careers/program-streams/analytics-process-graduate-stream-with-nab) - A consulting setuo where the clients are all internal with all of the bamking data at your fingertips. Staff made a good sell of diversity of projects
-   [Cognizant](https://www.cognizant.com/artificial-intelligence) - has an AI dept and is pretty big
