I've recently been asked about my experience and reccomendations in the space of UX design. While I always prioritise usability of applications, I definitely have an approach to User Experience centred around Web technology and in creating technology solutions that help people with their lives.

However, I know this is overly general and I have compiled the following from trusted colleagues, my own research and some prior reading
